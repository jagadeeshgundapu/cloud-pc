ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Computing without boundaries.

A Cloud PC concept that makes powerful computing accessible from different devices.

Your desktop, delivered through the cloud.

Cloud PC is designed to let users access a desktop environment through a browser on a laptop, desktop, tablet or mobile device. Instead of depending entirely on expensive local hardware, users can select a computing configuration based on their requirements.

The concept is intended for users who need additional computing power for productivity, development, gaming, video editing and other demanding workloads without owning a high-end physical computer.

Centralized data concept

Keep computing resources and files in a managed environment.
